Lighters and Their Uses

The Lighter has many uses. Many of which include:

  1. Lighting candles
  2. Starting campfires or grills
  3. Igniting fireworks
  4. Lighting cigarettes or cigars
  5. Creating a flame for survival situations
  6. Heating up metal for bending or shaping

These are just a handful of the many wonderful uses of lighters.
